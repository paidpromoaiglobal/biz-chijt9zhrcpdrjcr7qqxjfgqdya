About Us

SANEKI SILK HOUSE

Saneki Silk House is a premier destination for exquisite silk and ethnic fabrics, proudly serving the community of Jorhat, Assam. Located in the heart of Old Balibat near SBI ATM, opposite Purona Masjid in Fancy Ali, we have built a reputation for offering an unmatched selection of high-quality silks, mekhela chadors, sarees, and traditional Indian textiles that celebrate the rich weaving heritage of Assam and beyond.

With a stellar rating of 4.4 out of 5, our customers trust us for genuine quality, fair pricing, and warm personalized service. Whether you are shopping for a festive occasion, a wedding, or simply looking to add a touch of traditional elegance to your wardrobe, Saneki Silk House is your go-to store. We are open six days a week from 9:00 AM to 8:30 PM and on Sundays from 9:30 AM to 7:30 PM, making it convenient for every shopper to visit us.
